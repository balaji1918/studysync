Why StudySync?

Hard topics, nobody to ask

It is not always easy to find someone who can explain a concept in a way that makes sense to you.

Hard to find study partners

Classmates who could help you are out there, but you rarely know who is strong in the subjects you are weak in.

Hard to build a project team

Good teams need the right mix of skills and roles, and finding that mix takes time.

Three ways StudySync helps

Explain My Doubt

Understand difficult topics through peer explanations.

Find My Study Partner

Connect with classmates who complement your learning needs.

Find My Project Team

Discover projects and build teams with complementary skills.

How it works

  1. 1

    Create your profile

    Tell us your college, what you can help with, and what you want to learn.

  2. 2

    Ask, match, or post

    Ask a question, look for a study partner, or post a project that needs teammates.

  3. 3

    Work together

    Explain concepts, plan study sessions, and keep your project tasks organised in one place.

Built for students

  • Learn from classmates who recently studied the same material.
  • Teach what you know and strengthen your own understanding.
  • Meet people with skills that complement yours.
  • Keep questions, study plans and project work connected.
