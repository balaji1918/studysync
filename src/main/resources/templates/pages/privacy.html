Privacy

What StudySync stores

Your account email, a hashed (not readable) password, the profile details you enter, and the content you create on the site.

What other students can see

Other students can see your profile details (name, college, department, year, subjects, skills and interests) only if you allow discovery in your profile settings. Your email address and password are never shown to other students.

Your choices

You can edit your profile at any time or hide it from other students. Please do not post phone numbers or other private contact details in public fields.

Cookies

StudySync uses a session cookie to keep you logged in. It is not used for advertising.

Site operators: review and adapt this page to your college's rules and local law before opening StudySync to real users.
